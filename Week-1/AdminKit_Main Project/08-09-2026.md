"No content is removed at narrow width" means that when a website is viewed on a smaller screen—like a smartphone or a narrow web browser window—all of the original information, text, images, and features remain available to the user.

Instead of hiding or cutting out parts of the desktop version to fit the smaller screen, the website dynamically rearranges, stacks, or scales its design elements to ensure mobile users do not miss out on any content. This approach is a core principle of responsive web design and mobile-accessibility.
