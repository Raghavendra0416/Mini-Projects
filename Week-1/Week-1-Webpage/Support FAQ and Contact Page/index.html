Where can I find an invoice?

The invoice is found in the Payments Page and Open Billing, then choose Invoices by searching the required invoices.

Can I change my plan?

Plan can be changed before Payment. If the Payment is completed Plan cannot be changed. After payment, The Plan can only be changed by contacting support.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Account Settings, select Subscription, then choose Cancel Subscription. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.
